## HEMU LIU / Dylan Liu

Hi — I'm **HEMU LIU** (刘赫木), also known as Dylan. I'm a Computer Science student at [SUSTech](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/) in Shenzhen, focusing on **HPC & AI**: multimodal foundation models, federated learning for medical imaging, and large-scale distributed systems.

你好，我是 **刘赫木（HEMU LIU / Dylan）**，在南方科技大学学习计算机科学，关注高性能计算与人工智能，尤其是多模态大模型、医疗影像联邦学习，以及大规模分布式系统。

I work with the [iMED](https://www.imed-lab.com/) lab on AI for ophthalmology, and I've competed in ASC / ISC / IndySCC and related HPC challenges. In summer 2024 I studied cloud-native systems at [NUS](https://nus.edu.sg/) with [Prof. Richard TB Ma](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/tbma/).

This blog is where I share research notes, travel, and technical experiments — a more open home than WeChat or Notion for friends around the world.
